About Our Mission & Math

Rooted deep in the rich soil of timeless spiritual traditions, the Maya Mahasakti Mission and Math serves as a sacred sanctuary for internal peace, dynamic spiritual development, and dedicated philanthropic labor.

Operating gracefully as both a monastic community (Math) and a modern non-governmental service organisation (Mission), we strive to seamlessly bridge the gap between contemplative spiritual practices and active, real-world benevolence.

Daridra Narayana Seva

Serving the divine present within the underprivileged, the sick, and the helpless.

Spiritual Enlightenment

Spreading wisdom through regular scriptural discourses, yoga, and meditation.

What We Do

Our Spiritual & Philanthropic Services

Following the noble footprints of traditional monastic orders, our activities encompass twin pillars of work: Spiritual Upliftment and NGO Humanitarian Relief.

Math Activities & Worship

Daily ritual prayers (Arati), spiritual discourses, celebrating holy anniversaries, and conducting extensive meditation retreats for sincere spiritual seekers.

Medical Philanthropy

Running charitable dispensaries, organising free diagnostic camps, and distributing crucial medicines to vulnerable rural and semi-urban families.

Educational Support

Providing essential textbooks, school uniforms, coaching facilities, and financial scholarships to bright, underprivileged children.

Disaster Relief & Aid

Standing strong on the frontlines to distribute food rations, clean water, blankets, and urgent rehabilitation materials during unexpected natural calamities.
